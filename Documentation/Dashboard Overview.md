# Retail Revenue Intelligence Dashboard

## Dashboard Overview

The [Retail Revenue Intelligence Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/views/RetailRevenueIntelligence/RetailRevenueIntelligenceDashboard?:language=en-US&:sid=&:redirect=auth&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link) is an interactive Tableau dashboard built using the [UCI Online Retail II dataset.](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/502/online%2Bretail)


The dashboard is designed to transform transaction level retail data into an executive friendly view of business performance. It allows users to explore revenue trends, customer performance, product performance, geographic performance, and other key areas of the business through interactive visualizations.

The purpose of the dashboard is to help business stakeholders quickly understand:

* How the business is performing overall
* How revenue changes over time
* Which customers contribute the most revenue
* Which products generate the most revenue
* How performance differs across countries
* Where potential opportunities or areas of concern may exist

## Data

The analysis uses the UCI Online Retail II dataset, containing transaction level records from a UK based online retailer.

The source data covers transactions from December 1, 2009 through December 9, 2011. The data was profiled and cleaned prior to being used for analysis.
Data Cutoff Note

Because the dataset ends on December 9, 2011, December 2011 represents only a partial month. The apparent decline at the end of the Monthly Net Revenue trend should therefore not be interpreted as a full month decline in business performance.


## Tool

The dashboard was developed using Tableau Public and is designed to provide an interactive, portfolio ready presentation of the analysis.
